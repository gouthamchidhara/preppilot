Support

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Contact us

Email support@preppilot.us and tell us what happened, what you were doing, and the email address on your PrepPilot account. We read every message. Do not send passwords or your full payment card details.

What Live AI includes

A Live AI session is 60 minutes in one go: a spoken practice test with a verdict, or an open conversation with AI. Pro includes two 30-minute Live AI sessions a month. A one-time session is a separate purchase that works on Free or Pro and does not use Pro's monthly minutes. If your connection drops, rejoin within the hour and you pick up the same session with the time that is left. You are not charged again.

Cancel Pro

Open your Apple ID subscription settings (iPhone: Settings, then your name, then Subscriptions; or App Store, then your account, then Subscriptions), find PrepPilot and choose Cancel Subscription. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for. Cancelling in the app is not possible; subscriptions are managed by Apple.

Restore purchases

If Pro or a Live AI session you bought is not showing, first check you are signed in to PrepPilot with the same account and to the App Store with the same Apple ID you bought with. Then go to Profile and tap Restore purchases. Purchases restore automatically when you sign in with the same account. If it still does not appear, email support@preppilot.us with your purchase receipt from Apple.

Delete your account

Go to Profile and tap Delete account. This removes your email, name, plan and paid usage counters from your PrepPilot account. It does not cancel a Pro subscription — cancel that separately in your Apple ID subscription settings first, or it will keep renewing. To stop free-trial abuse we retain a pseudonymous sign-in identifier, deletion time and how much of the free trial was used. See the Privacy Policy for the full detail.

Refunds

All payments are processed by Apple, so refunds go through Apple too: sign in at reportaproblem.apple.com, find the PrepPilot purchase and choose Request a Refund. PrepPilot cannot refund an App Store purchase directly.
