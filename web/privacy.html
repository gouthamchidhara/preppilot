Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

What stays on your device

Your study profile (target role, background, self-ratings, interview date), flashcard progress, chat history, generated card sets and any private packs you import are stored only on your device. You can export or erase them from Profile → Data.

What we store on our servers

If you sign in (Google or Apple), we store your account identifier, email address, name (if provided), sign-in dates, your plan and your AI usage counters (messages and voice seconds). For voice and Live AI sessions, we also store call identifiers, the associated account identifier, reserved duration, start and end times, plan and call status. We use this to run your account, enforce plan and session limits and prevent abuse.

AI features

When you use AI chat, the text you send, a short summary of your study profile and the card you are viewing are sent to Microsoft Azure OpenAI to generate a reply. If you are signed in and request role suggestions, your pasted experience or résumé summary is also sent to Azure OpenAI for that request; PrepPilot does not save that pasted summary on its servers.

During a voice or Live AI session, microphone audio is streamed to Azure OpenAI for the length of the session. In "During your interview" mode, that audio can include other people on the call. Get their permission before sharing their audio and follow the interviewer's rules. PrepPilot does not record or store session audio on its servers. Azure OpenAI does not use customer data to train models.

"Fresh from the field" scenarios are generated for a selected role and cached on our servers. Their generation does not include your account details, résumé summary or other personal profile data.

Guide suggestions

If you submit a guide suggestion while signed in, we store its text, topic, account identifier, email address and submission time in Azure Table Storage so the team can review it. When email delivery is configured, the suggestion and your email address are also emailed to the team through Azure Communication Services. Do not include passwords, confidential employer information or other sensitive information in a suggestion.

Purchases

Subscriptions and one-time Live AI purchases are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status and purchased session credits, using your PrepPilot account identifier and store transaction information. We never see your payment card details.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete account removes your email, name, plan and paid usage counters from your account record. To stop free-trial abuse we retain a pseudonymous sign-in identifier, deletion time and how much of the free trial was used. This does not automatically erase previously submitted guide suggestions, team emails or call metadata used to enforce session time limits. Store and RevenueCat purchase records are managed by those providers. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google; deleting your PrepPilot account does not cancel them.

Contact

Questions: open an issue in the PrepPilot issue tracker. Issues are public; do not post personal details, résumé text, passwords or purchase receipts.
