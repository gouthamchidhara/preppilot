Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 16, 2026

What stays on your device

Your study profile (target role, background, self-ratings, interview date), flashcard progress, chat history, generated card sets and any private packs you import are stored only on your device. You can export or erase them from Profile → Data.

What we store on our servers

If you sign in (Google or Apple), we store your account identifier, email address, name (if provided), sign-in dates, your plan and your AI usage counters (messages and voice seconds). We use this only to run your account, enforce plan limits and prevent abuse.

AI features

When you use the AI coach, the text you send, a short summary of your study profile and the card you are viewing are sent to Microsoft Azure OpenAI to generate a reply. In voice mode your microphone audio is streamed to Azure OpenAI for the duration of the session. We do not store your chat messages or recordings on our servers. Azure OpenAI does not use customer data to train models.

Purchases

Subscriptions are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription status. We never see your payment card details.

Deleting your data

Profile → Delete account removes your email, name, plan and usage history from our servers immediately. To stop free-trial abuse we keep only your anonymous sign-in identifier and how much of the free trial was used. Subscriptions are managed by Apple or Google; cancel them in your device's subscription settings.
